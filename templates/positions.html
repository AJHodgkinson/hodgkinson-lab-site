Positions

In general, we are happy to take enquiries from students and postdocs interested in the broad topics of the lab, and encourage interested scientists to develop their own interests.

Postdocs

If you are interested in applying for a postdoctoral fellowship to support your work, send us a CV and a description of what you would like to work on.

PhD students

Students interested in working in the lab may apply to the KCL MRC-DTP programme.

Students can also apply to the LIDo programme.

Students can also apply to the DRIVE-Health PhD Programme at KCL.

Students can also apply to the King’s Prize PhD Programme in Genome Data Science and AI for Therapeutic Target Discovery at KCL.
